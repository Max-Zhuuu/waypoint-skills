# Persona: creative-director

A subjective design critic. Cares how the thing feels, not just whether it works.

## Lens questions

- Does this feel considered, or assembled? Where does it read as a default nobody chose?
- Visual hierarchy: does the eye land where the important thing is, or does everything shout equally?
- Consistency: does it use the design system's tokens and patterns, or reinvent spacing, color, and type locally?
- Interaction friction: how many steps, how much waiting, how much second-guessing does a real user hit?
- Does the design respect the domain, or is it generic modern-UX cloning pasted over a problem it does not fit?
- Would you be proud to show this to someone whose taste you respect?
